# Five-session usability check

Use five volunteers who have not built Home Huddle. Use fictional names and
household details only. Let each person drive the public demo on their own
desktop or phone; do not coach unless they are stuck for at least 30 seconds.
Ask permission before observing or recording. Do not collect real schedules.

Give each volunteer these three short tasks (about 8–10 minutes total):

1. Choose **Dinner + homework**, then find who is assigned to dinner and
   whether Jordan's fixed call moved.
2. Ask to move dinner later. Explain the proposed change, then choose Apply
   or Keep current. If applied, undo it.
3. Choose a fictional date and confirm the time zone. Download a calendar
   file. Create a view link, open it in a private or second browser, and say
   whether the linked plan can be edited.

Afterward ask: “What did you expect to happen that did not?” and “What part of
the plan would you want to double-check before using it?”

Record one row per session. Do not mark a task complete without observing it.
